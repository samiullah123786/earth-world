# Neighborhood workshop surface brief

Scope: `/workshop.html`, a neighborhood planning and owned-plot publication editor; Operate mode with a real 3D neighborhood as the central work surface. Audience: the Earth owner and external owner-bound agents preparing layouts. Existing live world stays intact.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A neighborhood made from inspectable, reusable blocks. A fixed angled camera makes spatial editing understandable without free-flight controls.

OWN-WORLD: Earthfolk cream, timber, warm roofs, sage terrain and dark green controls. Professional existing CC0 models, actual rendered thumbnails, compact native controls, and generous space for the neighborhood. Segoe UI is a deliberate workhorse face for the Windows/browser editor, not a decorative display identity.

STORY: The owner starts from a reproducible draft, edits and saves locally, then gives an external brain the approved catalogue. Imported plans are validated and remain local planning documents.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim header, 304px desktop tool rail, and a large angled neighborhood with visible houses, connecting paths, trees, and clearly labeled sample people. Camera and history controls sit below the map. On phones, the whole neighborhood appears above the tool rail without cropping.

FORM: Code-led world editor, pinned by the owner's approved modular 3D direction. Prior exploration seed b6a205fc is superseded by explicit owner direction; no approved image comp. Signature interaction: choose a model, preview its grid footprint, then confirm placement with reversible history.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: local generation/import never publishes; live structural proposals require an existing owner session and follow the same Kernel geometry, fees, owner review, and civic review as signed agents. No public registrations, central LLM, arbitrary model imports, or permission claims from imported data. Existing EarthForge approved raster sources are untouched. Generated thumbnails are runtime renders of existing Kenney assets.

Approved extension: the owner can connect their existing citizen, select one draft home/community model, inspect its canonical footprint at absolute owned-plot coordinates, request a permit, and explicitly approve or decline it. Live publication stays subordinate to the neighborhood canvas. Landscape drafts remain local.
