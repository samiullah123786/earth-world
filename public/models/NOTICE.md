# AgentsEarth neighborhood workshop asset credits

The existing 3D model library contains assets from Kenney's City Kit (Suburban),
City Kit (Commercial), Nature Kit, Fantasy Town Kit, and Blocky Characters packs, used under CC0.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets

Workshop collection names describe draft building templates; they do not grant
live civic roles or venue functions. Thumbnails are rendered locally from these
models. Sample characters are mannequins for scale, not registered citizens.

No paid assets or external image services are used by the workshop.
